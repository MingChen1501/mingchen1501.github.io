*This year I've completed two full 12-year cycles — time flies, I'll be old before I know it. Here's my Tet holiday diary.*

## I only got off work on the 27th of Tet and went home to celebrate with family; on the 28th I finally had time for a haircut, buying peach and kumquat trees, and cleaning the house (┬┬﹏┬┬)

![HairCut](/assets/img/2023-01-31-tet-nguyen-dan-quy-mao-2023-cua-toi/hair-cut.webp){: width="900" height="1600" }
I grew my hair out for Tet this year (❁´◡`❁).
![HairDye](/assets/img/2023-01-31-tet-nguyen-dan-quy-mao-2023-cua-toi/hair-dye.webp){: width="899" height="1599" }
After the cut I bleached it a bit to make the color pop.
The bleaching process deserves to be called painful. First I bleached it blonde, then added a violet tint — but I had to rinse before the dye had fully set to take my family peach-tree shopping, so the color didn't turn out as planned. Then I decided to bleach once more for a smoky gray. Three rounds of bleach in total, and my scalp hurt like never before U_U
