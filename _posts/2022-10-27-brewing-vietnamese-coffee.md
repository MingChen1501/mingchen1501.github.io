>A good cup of coffee has to be both sweet and bitter ![Step 2](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b2.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* Add coffee grounds to fill half of the phin filter.

![Step 1](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b1.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* Place the press plate into the phin and press down gently (pressing force guided by the ancestors :D).

![Step 2](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b2.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* Pour in water to fill 2/3 of the phin and let it bloom until the water has soaked fully into the grounds.

![Step 3](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b3.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* Fill the phin to the top with water and wait for it to finish dripping.

![Step 4](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b4.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* The result: a cup of pure black coffee — strong enough to keep you wide awake :D :D :D (/≧▽≦)/.

![Step 5](/assets/img/2022-10-27-cach-pha-ca-phe/b5.jpg){: width="899" height="1600" }

* Add sugar, fresh milk, condensed milk and ice to taste.
