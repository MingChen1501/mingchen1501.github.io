Hi, I'm **Tran Cong Minh** (aka **Ming Chen**) — the author of this little blog.

This blog is where I jot down bits of my studies, work and life: tinkering with Linux, trying out programming tools, trips, Tet holidays with family and friends. I write mostly in Vietnamese, in a personal-diary style — whatever comes to mind, unpolished but always honest.

Professionally, I work in software and enjoy exploring new technologies, especially ones that make everyday work more effective. Beyond computers, I love coffee, hiking and getting out and about whenever I get the chance.

If you find a post useful, or want to share feedback, feel free to reach out by email at **mingchen1501@gmail.com** or find me on the social links in the footer. I always enjoy hearing from readers.

Thanks for stopping by, and have a lovely day!

## Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers of its own. Your theme choice is saved only in this browser's `localStorage` (key `theme`) — clear it anytime via your browser's site-data settings. Comments on post pages load [Giscus](https://giscus.app) (GitHub Discussions) and follow GitHub's privacy terms once you interact with them. Pages are served over TLS through Cloudflare's edge network, which also handles caching and email-address obfuscation.
