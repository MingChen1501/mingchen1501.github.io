{%- comment -%} Sidebar shared by the list pages (#58, reworked in #63): the home list (`/`, `/page2/`…) in `_layouts/home.html` and the tag pages (`/tags//`) in `_layouts/tag.html`. Both layouts render this same include, so the two blocks exist once: 1. the highlighted posts: the posts that opt in with `highlight: true` in their own front matter (`_posts/*.md`), newest first, at most five, each with its thumbnail (when it has one), its title and its publication date, 2. every tag as a collapsed row (`
` without `open`): the tag chip and how many posts it has, and — once the reader opens it — at most three post links plus a link to that tag's page. `site.posts` — not `paginator.posts` — on purpose: the highlights are the same on every page, so the sidebar does not change while paging. No post marked yet (a fresh site, or every highlighted post removed): the block falls back to the five newest posts and its heading says so, so the sidebar is never an empty box. Height budget (#63): the sidebar has to fit beside the list without a scrollbar of its own, so every row here is deliberately short — a capped thumbnail with a two-line title, and collapsed tag rows. The styles that bound it are in `_sass/minima/custom-styles.scss`; `tests/e2e/test_sidebar_layout.py` guards both this content and the absent inner scroll, and the dates use the compact `%d-%m-%Y` because `site.minima.date_format` (the verbose Vietnamese string used on the home list and post pages) does not fit a 300px column. Thumbnail resolution: `post.image`, else the first image of the post body, else no thumbnail at all (no placeholder art). No `thumb-*` files are generated: `assets/img/` runs close to its 6MB budget (see `script/check-images`). Chips come from `_includes/post-tags.html`, the single source of the chip markup (#45/#47); it takes a one-element tag list here.
