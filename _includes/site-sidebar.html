Nothing in the file resolves (a fresh site, an empty or missing file, or every listed slug unknown/unpublished): the block falls back to the five newest posts and its heading says so, so the sidebar is never an empty box. Height budget (#63): the sidebar has to fit beside the list without a scrollbar of its own, so every row here is deliberately short — a capped thumbnail with a two-line title, and collapsed tag rows. Thumbnail resolution: `post.image`, else the first image of the post body, else no thumbnail at all (no placeholder art). No `thumb-*` files are generated: `assets/img/` runs close to its 6MB budget (see `script/check-images`). Chips come from `_includes/post-tags.html`, the single source of the chip markup (#45/#47); it takes a one-element tag list here.
