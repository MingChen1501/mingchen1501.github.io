{%- comment -%} Sidebar shared by the list pages (#58): the home list (`/`, `/page2/`…) in `_layouts/home.html` and the tag pages (`/tags//`) in `_layouts/tag.html`. Both layouts render this same include, so the two blocks exist once: 1. the three most recent posts of the whole site — thumbnail when the post has one, plus a short description, 2. every tag with its posts (`
`, expanded by default, at most five posts each, then a link to that tag's page). `site.posts` — not `paginator.posts` — on purpose: the three posts are the same on every page, so the sidebar does not change while paging. Thumbnail resolution: `post.image`, else the first image of the post body, else no thumbnail at all (no placeholder art). No `thumb-*` files are generated: `assets/img/` runs close to its 6MB budget (see `script/check-images`). Chips come from `_includes/post-tags.html`, the single source of the chip markup (#45/#47); it takes a one-element tag list here. Locals are `sidebar_*`-prefixed so nothing this include assigns can collide with the caller's variables. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sidebar_date_format = site.minima.date_format | default: "%b %-d, %Y" -%}
