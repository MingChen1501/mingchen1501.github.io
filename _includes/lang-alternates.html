{%- comment -%} hreflang alternates (#93). Every content page/post carries a shared `ref` across its English and Vietnamese versions; both sides are resolved by the shared `ref-urls.html` partial (over `site.posts`, the `site.vi_posts` collection and `site.pages`) and this include emits absolute `en` / `vi` alternates plus `x-default` pointing at English, the default language. Pages without a `ref` (404, feeds, sitemap, standalone demo pages) get no alternates. Canonicals stay with jekyll-seo-tag. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign alt_ref = page.ref | default: '' -%} {%- if alt_ref != '' -%} {%- assign alt_en = '' -%} {%- assign alt_vi = '' -%} {%- comment -%} Paginated list pages (`/page2/`…) carry the home page's `ref`; their translation relationship is home <-> home, so point both sides (and the x-default) at the first pages, never at a page-N URL. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if paginator and paginator.page and paginator.page > 1 -%} {%- assign alt_en = '/' -%} {%- assign alt_vi = '/vi/' -%} {%- else -%} {%- include ref-urls.html ref=alt_ref -%} {%- assign alt_en = ref_en -%} {%- assign alt_vi = ref_vi -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if alt_en != '' -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if alt_vi != '' -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if alt_en != '' -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
